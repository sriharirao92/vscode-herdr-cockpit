# Reading the sidebar

- **Spaces** are cards: click one to open its folder and every agent and shell as terminal tabs.
- **Agents** show a colored status: working, blocked (needs you), done, idle.
- **Needs you** lists blocked and done agents with a preview of their screen.
- **Shells** show what is running, or the last command and its output.

The `?` in the sidebar's title bar opens the full guide.
